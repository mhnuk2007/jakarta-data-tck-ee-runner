# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want this project to be a welcoming, respectful, and productive environment for everyone who participates.

Contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and users are expected to communicate professionally and constructively.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

* Being respectful and considerate.
* Welcoming different viewpoints and experiences.
* Giving constructive technical feedback.
* Focusing discussions on the technical subject.
* Assuming good intent while resolving disagreements.
* Accepting reasonable corrections.
* Helping others understand project decisions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

* Harassment or discrimination.
* Personal attacks or insults.
* Threats or intimidation.
* Deliberate disruption of technical discussions.
* Publishing private information without permission.
* Trolling or intentionally inflammatory behavior.
* Other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or hostile.

## Technical Disagreements

Technical disagreement is expected in an open-source project.

When disagreements occur:

1. Focus on the code, documentation, or technical proposal.
2. Explain the reasoning behind your position.
3. Provide evidence or reproducible examples where possible.
4. Avoid personal criticism.
5. Respect the final decision of maintainers when a decision is required.

## Reporting Problems

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the project maintainer rather than escalating the situation publicly.

For security-related matters, follow the process described in `SECURITY.md`.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take reasonable action to address behavior that violates this Code of Conduct.

Actions may include:

* Requesting changes to communication.
* Removing inappropriate comments or content.
* Closing discussions that become abusive or unproductive.
* Restricting participation when necessary.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project repositories, issue discussions, pull requests, reviews, and other project-related interactions.

## Attribution

This document is intended to establish practical expectations for respectful participation in this project.
